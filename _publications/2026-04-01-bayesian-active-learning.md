We introduce an on-the-fly Bayesian active-learning framework that makes the first-principles prediction of challenging phase transitions computationally affordable, by training machine-learning interatomic potentials only where the underlying model is uncertain.

Preprint available at [arXiv:2604.25756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25756).
