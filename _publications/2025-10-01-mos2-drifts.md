Combined experimental and first-principles study showing that diffuse reflectance infrared spectroscopy can be used as a rapid, non-destructive probe of the quality and purity of MoS2 nanosheet dispersions. Simulated infrared spectra were obtained with [aiida-vibroscopy](https://aiida-vibroscopy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
