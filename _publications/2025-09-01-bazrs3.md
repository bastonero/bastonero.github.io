A joint experimental and computational investigation of the chalcogenide perovskite BaZrS3, connecting its strong luminescence to the underlying electronic structure and lattice dynamics. First-principles phonons and Raman spectra were computed with [aiida-vibroscopy](https://aiida-vibroscopy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
