Generalized active-learning workflows for the automated training of machine-learning interatomic potentials, interfacing the [NequIP](https://github.com/mir-group/nequip), [Allegro](https://github.com/mir-group/allegro), and [FLARE](https://github.com/mir-group/flare) codes with first-principles calculations.

The package implements uncertainty-quantification and deduplication strategies to select, on-the-fly, only the configurations that are actually informative, making first-principles thermodynamics and phase transitions affordable.

_Currently a private repository._
