A multi-author community roadmap surveying how artificial intelligence is transforming scientific research, and outlining the open challenges and future directions across a broad range of disciplines. Our contribution covers the role of AI and automation in atomistic simulations of materials.

Preprint available at [arXiv:2511.20976](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20976).
